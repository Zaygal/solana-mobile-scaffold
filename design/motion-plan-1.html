Motion Plan

No code changes made · for your decision
The diagnosis. The rebuild is architecturally mobile and behaviourally inert. Every state change is a cut: the streak appears with no arrival, a button press is acknowledged by nothing, a sheet replaces the screen rather than rising over it. Layout is not what makes Snapchat, Telegram or Duolingo feel alive — every touch answering back is. That is the missing layer, and it is not colour or rounding.

01 · The motion law — three rules that decide every case below

R1

Motion answers touch. It never fills time.

If an animation runs while the user is doing nothing, it is decoration. Nothing loops. No idle pulse, no shimmer on a screen with nothing loading.

R2

The measurement stays linear.

The 10s hold and the streak count-up use linear timing, because easing implies a curve where there is only elapsed time. Everything else may be expressive.

R3

Celebrate only what is true.

A seal earns a moment. A day that was not sealed gets nothing. No confetti, no fire, no number that moves without a chain write. This is the honesty rule, as motion.
