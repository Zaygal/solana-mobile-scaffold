The principle. A native app is not distinguished by its layout. It is distinguished by the fact that nothing happens without a response. Below is every surface where a finger can land, what it does back, and how long it takes. Where a number is quoted, it is the actual value.

06 · Borrowed — the pattern, never the branding

Duolingo
A streak presented as a record with a stated cost, not a reward. Taken: the record framing. Refused: the owl, the urgency, the guilt.
Telegram
Sheets that follow your finger and can be thrown away. Taken: gesture physics. This is the single biggest gap in our build.
X
The action lands before the network answers, then reconciles — and visibly undoes itself. Taken wholesale for the seal row.
Snapchat
First gesture is the product, zero onboarding ceremony. Taken: as a warning. We are the opposite kind of product, so we keep the onboarding.
WhatsApp
A send feels physical — one tick, no animation you could name. Taken: the discipline of one small unambiguous acknowledgement.

07 · What I would refuse to animate

A flame or fire that grows — implies heat, energy, a body. We track a held button.
Any number moving without a chain write — the count-up must end on the real value.
Confetti, particles, bursts — celebration sized to something that did not happen.
Skeleton shimmer on Today — nothing is loading there. Fake latency is fake.
Any looping ambient motion — R1. It is the clearest tell of decoration.
A haptic on a failed action — feedback that contradicts the outcome.
Motion before the wallet sheet — not one frame of delay on the approval.
A 7-day celebration for 6 days — the milestone fires on the on-chain value only.
